# Mission: System Design (backend · infra · frontend)

> Confirmed via the defaults (senior fullstack; fundamentals-first; interview +
> real-world in parallel; frontend track included in Phase 3).

## Why

Become the engineer who can **design systems that hold up under scale and
failure**, and who can **pass a system-design interview with a structured,
tradeoff-driven approach** — across backend, infrastructure, and frontend. The
concrete outcome: you lead a real design doc (requirements → constraints →
tradeoffs → architecture) instead of improvising, and you walk into a
system-design loop calm, because you've rehearsed the opening moves.

## Success looks like

- Open any system-design interview with the **clarify → estimate → high-level →
  deep-dive → tradeoffs** script, and drive it rather than react to it.
- Given a real feature, name the **forces in tension** (latency vs throughput,
  consistency vs availability, cost vs reliability, coupling vs autonomy) and
  justify a choice with numbers.
- Reason fluently about the building blocks: load balancers, databases
  (replication/sharding/indexing), caches, queues, CDNs, object storage — and
  where each breaks.
- Design across the stack: a backend service, the infra to run/scale/observe it,
  and a frontend architecture (rendering strategy, data fetching, state) that
  fits the constraints.
- Write a one-page design that a senior reviewer considers sound.

## Constraints

- Learner is an **experienced developer** — skip intro CS; assume comfort with
  one backend stack, HTTP, SQL, and basic cloud.
- **Fundamentals-first**, breadth across the three layers (backend/infra/FE),
  then interview practice once the base is solid.
- Lessons follow the **repo house style**: graphical, self-citing, dark theme,
  **bilingual PT/EN** (default PT, EN one click away) — match `topics/pi`.
- Ponytail sensibility: teach the smallest mental model that explains the most;
  no vendor fads, no certification tracks.

## Out of scope (for now)

- Distributed-systems **theory proofs** (Paxos/Raft at the math level) — we use
  the *consequences*, not the proofs.
- **LeetCode / coding** interview prep (separate topic).
- **Vendor certification** tracks (AWS/Azure certs).
- Mobile/embedded system design.
