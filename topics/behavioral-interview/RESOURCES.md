# Behavioral Interview Resources

> Curated, high-trust sources. Lessons draw from these, not parametric guesses.

## Knowledge — the techniques

- [BLUF (communication) — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BLUF_%28communication%29)
  Bottom Line Up Front: the conclusion first, then the detail. Use for: the *headline-first* habit that makes answers sound senior.
- [U.S. Army AR 25-50 — Army Correspondence (the doctrine BLUF comes from)](https://home.army.mil/wood/application/files/3015/5751/8343/AR_25_50_Army_Correspondence.pdf)
  The military origin of "bottom line up front." Use for: citing the pedigree of the technique.
- [Situation, Task, Action, Result — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Situation%2C_task%2C_action%2C_result)
  STAR: the standard structured-interview answering framework. Use for: the proof layer under your BLUF headline.
- [The Behavioral Interview — Yale Office of Career Strategy](https://ocs.yale.edu/channels/the-behavioral-interview/)
  What behavioral questions are and why interviewers use them. Use for: understanding the premise ("past behavior predicts future performance").
- [STAR Method of Interviewing — UW Career & Internship Center](https://careers.uw.edu/resources/star-method-of-interviewing/)
  Practical STAR walkthrough. Use for: examples of structured answers.

## Knowledge — what they ask (senior/fullstack)

- [Senior Software Engineer Interview Questions — Indeed](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/interviewing/senior-software-engineer-interview-questions)
  Use for: the common senior-engineer behavioral themes (leadership, conflict, delivery).
- [Software Engineer Behavioral Interview Questions — Indeed](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/interviewing/software-engineer-behavioral-interview-questions)
  Use for: question bank seeds.
- [Amazon SDE-III Interview Prep — amazon.jobs](https://www.amazon.jobs/content/en/how-we-hire/sde-iii-interview-prep)
  The gold-standard senior behavioral bar (Leadership Principles, depth of probing). Use for: calibrating to a rigorous senior bar — they probe your stories hard.

## Knowledge — sounding natural, not scripted

- [Practice for Interviews — Santa Clara University Career Center](https://www.scu.edu/careercenter/toolkit/practice-for-interviews/)
  Prepare stories, not scripts; practice aloud. Use for: the "rehearse, don't memorize" principle.

## Wisdom (communities)

- [r/ExperiencedDevs](https://reddit.com/r/ExperiencedDevs)
  Senior engineers debriefing real interviews. Use for: what actually gets asked and what sinks candidates at senior level.
