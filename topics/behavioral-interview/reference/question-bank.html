Ownership

"Tell me about the project you're most proud of."

Tests: ownership, impact, what you value. The #1 most-asked. Have a killer ready.

BLUF hook: "I'm most proud of ___ because it ___ (impact in numbers)."

Ownership

"Tell me about a time you took initiative beyond your role."

Tests: proactivity, ownership beyond your lane.

BLUF hook: "I noticed ___ was broken/no one owned it, so I ___."

Ownership

"Describe something you owned end-to-end."

Tests: can you take a thing from problem → shipped → maintained.

Conflict

"Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate."

Tests: ego, listening, how you resolve technical conflict. Avoid "I was right, they were wrong."

BLUF hook: "We disagreed on ___; I ___ (sought data/listened); we landed on ___."

Conflict

"Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager or PM."

Tests: disagree-and-commit, upward communication, respect.

BLUF hook: "I pushed back on ___ with __ (evidence); once decided, I committed fully."

Conflict

"Tell me about a time you had to convince the team of a technical direction."

Tests: influence through evidence, not authority.

Failure

"Tell me about a time you failed or made a serious mistake."

Tests: self-awareness, blame posture, learning. Real failure + real lesson. No "I work too hard."

BLUF hook: "I broke ___; the root cause was ___; here's what I changed so it can't recur."

Failure

"Tell me about a production incident you caused or handled."

Tests: incident maturity, blameless post-mortem thinking, observability.

Failure

"Tell me about a project that didn't go as planned."

Tests: course-correction, honesty, what you'd do differently.

Ambiguity

"Describe a project where the requirements were unclear."

Tests: senior-defining skill — can you define the problem, not just solve a defined one?

BLUF hook: "The goal was clear but the path wasn't; I drove clarity by ___ (proto/data/talking to users)."

Ambiguity

"Tell me about a decision you made with incomplete information."

Tests: judgment under uncertainty, reversible-vs-irreversible thinking.

Ambiguity

"How do you prioritize competing demands?"

Tests: prioritization framework, saying no, stakeholder management.

Delivery

"Tell me about a time you delivered under a tight deadline."

Tests: scope management, what you cut, calm under pressure.

BLUF hook: "We shipped ___ on time by ruthlessly cutting ___; the tradeoff was worth it because ___."

Delivery

"Tell me about a time you had to ship a tradeoff (speed vs quality)."

Tests: pragmatic engineering, not perfectionism. Name what you gave up.

Delivery

"Tell me about a time you improved performance/scalability."

Tests: depth, measurement, before/after numbers.

Mentorship

"Tell me about a time you mentored someone."

Tests: multiplier effect, seniority, patience.

BLUF hook: "I helped ___ grow from ___ to ___; the team benefit was ___."

Mentorship

"Tell me about a time you helped a struggling teammate."

Tests: empathy + directness, giving hard feedback.

Mentorship

"Tell me about a time you improved a team process or culture."

Tests: systems thinking, lifting the whole team, not just yourself.

Influence

"Tell me about a time you aligned cross-functional stakeholders."

Tests: communication across PM/design/other teams, negotiation.

Influence

"Tell me about a time you explained a complex technical issue to non-technical people."

Tests: BLUF in the wild — can you make the complex simple?

BLUF hook: Literally lead with the bottom line. This question is the BLUF test.

Influence

"Tell me about a time you influenced without authority."

Tests: persuasion via data/trust/relationships, not title.

The one-question-from-each-category rule: if you prepare one strong story for each of these 7 categories, you can answer ~90% of behavioral questions by reframing. Same story, different angle. That's the whole game.
