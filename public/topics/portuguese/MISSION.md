# Mission: Português

## Why

I already speak Portuguese — I live in Brazil — but my dream is to sound
**native**, not like a Spanish speaker who happens to speak Portuguese. My
Spanish is so close that it sabotages me from behind: too many "false
positives" — words and structures that feel right because they exist in
Spanish, but mean something else (or don't exist) in Portuguese.

## Success looks like

- Speak daily Brazilian Portuguese without portuñol slips.
- Catch my own interference errors **before** Brazilians politely ignore them.
- Use the right word when Spanish offers a tempting look-alike
  (falsos amigos like _esquisito_, _apelido_, _largo_).
- Build a lifelong habit of short targeted drills, not passive immersion
  (immersion alone stopped working — it's what fossilized the errors).

## Constraints

- Contrastive Spanish → Portuguese teaching: every lesson anchors on the
  Spanish reflex I already have and retrains it.
- Short, interactive lessons with immediate feedback; real-world micro-tasks
  in my daily life in Brazil.
- Brazilian Portuguese only.

## Out of scope

- European Portuguese.
- Literary/formal written register.
- Language certifications.
