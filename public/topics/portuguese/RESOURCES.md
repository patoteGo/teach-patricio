# Português Resources

Curated for a Spanish speaker chasing native-like **Brazilian** Portuguese.
Priority: sources built specifically for the ES→PT interference problem.

## Knowledge

- [Tá Falado — Brazilian Portuguese for Speakers of Spanish (COERLL, UT Austin)](https://www.coerll.utexas.edu/brazilpod/tafalado/)
  46 free podcast lessons (24 pronunciation, 20 grammar) built around dialogs
  repeated in Portuguese AND Spanish for direct comparison. PDF transcripts
  included. **Primary source** for the phonetics phase (vowels, rhotics,
  intonation). coerll.utexas.edu
- [FSI — From Spanish to Portuguese: false cognates table](https://ielanguages.com/spanish-portuguese-false-cognates.html)
  Public-domain US Foreign Service Institute course module contrasting the
  classic false cognate pairs with correct equivalents. Data source for the
  falsos amigos reference.
- [Tradulex — Lista de falsos amigos português–espanhol](https://www.tradulex.com/Glossaries/falsos_por-esp.htm)
  Long curated ES↔PT false-friends glossary (extended by lusophile editors).
- [Brasilescola — Falsos cognatos em espanhol](https://brasilescola.uol.com.br/espanhol/falso-amigo.htm)
  Quick PT-oriented reference list with meanings in Portuguese.
- [Rojas (UnB, 2006) — Fossilização na interlíngua de hispanofalantes: "acomodação consentida"?](https://repositorio.unb.br/handle/10482/6657)
  Master's thesis explaining why Brazilians accommodate portuñol instead of
  correcting it, and why immersion alone fossilizes interference. Grounds the
  mission's core insight.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/Portuguese — "Where to learn PT" megathread](https://www.reddit.com/r/Portuguese/comments/1chlfur/where_to_learn_pt_the_megathread/)
  Active learner community; the megathread curates resources by level and
  goal. Good place to stress-test usage questions with natives.
- Daily life in Brazil is the real wisdom arena: the lessons' real-world
  micro-tasks (order, compliment, describe) ARE the community practice.
