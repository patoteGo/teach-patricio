# Subject is pre-launch: discovery mines the closest real past; bottleneck is likely demand-side

Correction to LR-0007: the sister-in-law baker is **also just starting — she has
no customers yet.** Therefore the order-handling interview questions (volume,
past orders, fulfillment pain) **don't apply** — there's no customer history to
observe.

## What this changes

- **Discovery adapts:** mine the **closest real past** instead — informal
  baking for family/friends, any inquiries/messages already received, and
  concrete actions she has already taken (Instagram, price list, Pix). The
  Mom-Test rule still holds (past over future, don't pitch), but the "past" is
  informal/pre-revenue, not order-volume.
- **The bottleneck is likely DEMAND-side, not fulfillment-side.** She has no
  orders to manage yet; the real barrier is getting found → receiving the first
  inquiries → quoting credibly → closing. **An "order-management dashboard"
  would be premature slop** (managing zero orders).
- **What EP likely builds for her changes:** a credible presence + a way to
  receive & respond to inquiries + consistent quoting — a demand/conversion
  tool, not a fulfillment tool. This is itself a sellable service for the many
  pre-launch small businesses.

## Implications

- Lesson 1 ("Antes do código") interview guide is **reworked for a pre-launch
  subject** (closest-real-past + demand-side barrier + readiness walkthrough).
- The eventual build (Lesson 2+) targets whatever the interview reveals is
  blocking her first customers — likely a presence/inquiry/quoting tool, not a
  dashboard.
