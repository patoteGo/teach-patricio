# New base: a real small business — the sister-in-law's bakery (human-grounded)

**The base is now a real person and a real, observed workflow** — replacing the
unvalidated EP-site marketing as the curriculum's foundation (see LR-0005
caveat). This is the most human-grounded base available.

The subject: the husband's **sister-in-law**, a skilled baker starting her
business (currently a nurse). Brazil context: high demand for festivity orders
(birthday cakes, party sweets, etc.).

**Her real, observed flow today:**

1. Instagram post (discovery / marketing)
2. Customer contacts her via WhatsApp (lead in)
3. She sees what she can offer + quotes a price (back-and-forth)
4. Agree on payment + delivery time (booking)
5. Deliver
6. Customer pays in **PIX** at the end

"There's room for some kind of software to facilitate the process" — a
**hypothesis, not yet a validated need.**

## Approach (proposed, pending user confirm): ground BEFORE build

Consistent with the user's own critique that the EP site was unvalidated
"slop": do **not** build software for an imagined need. The first step is
**customer discovery** — interview the baker (Mom-Test style) to map her real
workflow, find the **#1 bottleneck** (where she loses time / clients / money),
and confirm willingness to pay. Only then build to the real requirement. This
is the direct antidote to the slop problem and produces real requirements.

## Implications

- **Lesson 1 (proposed) = discovery interview with the baker.** Skill: run a
  truth-getting customer interview; map a real flow; find the bottleneck.
  Human-grounded; at Ellen's level; bridges build→sell. Primary source:
  _The Mom Test_ (Rob Fitzpatrick).
- The realtime app lesson ("A fonte da verdade", current 0001) becomes a
  **later** lesson, **re-grounded** in what the interview reveals (e.g. an
  order-tracking dashboard for the baker) — not scrapped.
- The sister-in-law is a **potential first paying customer** + first real case
  study → directly serves the "first paying customer" north star.
- **Open:** confirm the baker is willing to be interviewed / open to being a
  real first client; confirm Ellen can talk to her soon.
