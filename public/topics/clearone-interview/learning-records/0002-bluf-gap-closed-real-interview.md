# BLUF-first reflex CLOSED in the real interview

**Status:** active (supersedes LR-0001's gap finding)

The BLUF-first gap identified in LR-0001 — "never leads with BLUF" — **closed in the real
interview.** User confirmed: *"Yes, it lands. It feels different. It really set the tone of the
conversation."* The drill (5 reps + full answers) worked. This is now a habit, not a gap.

**Additional findings from the real interview:**

- The prep was **comprehensive** — *"nothing caught me off guard."* Every theme we prepped was
  either asked or naturally woven in.
- The interview was **conversational, not grilling** (30 min, engineering manager). The pitch
  answered several questions before they were asked — a clear win from BLUF-first.
- Senior empathy signal: the interviewer arrived late; the user was empathetic rather than
  annoyed — noted as a positive.
- **No technical/system-design segment** in this round.

**Implications for future sessions:**

- BLUF drill is no longer needed — the reflex is installed.
- Focus shifts to **next-round prep**: PO interview (conversational/product) and engineer peer
  (code thought process, not live coding).
