# System Design Interview Dojo Resources

> Practice resources are deliberately small. The goal is repetition with feedback, not collecting links.

## Knowledge

- [System Design Interview — Alex Xu / ByteByteGo](https://bytebytego.com/books/)
  Prompt patterns and worked examples; use after attempting a design, never before the first attempt.
- [Google SRE Workbook](https://sre.google/workbook/)
  SLOs, failure modes, and operational questions for deep dives.
- [A Philosophy of Software Design — John Ousterhout](https://web.stanford.edu/~ouster/cgi-bin/book.php)
  Information hiding and boundary quality; use to critique service/module cuts.

## Practice

- [System Design Primer](https://github.com/donnemartin/system-design-primer)
  Prompt bank and review checklist.
